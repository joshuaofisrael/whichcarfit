Sources and methods

Last checked 16 Nov 2026.

Owner's manuals

Battery type, wiper part or length, and bulb type come from that model year's owner's manual. The page number or the chart name is in the source note. If a chart lists two lamp packages, both stay on the page. If the manual does not print a figure, we do not borrow one from a parts counter, a forum, or another year.

NHTSA

Recall campaigns come from NHTSA's recalls API for that make, model, and model year. Complaint counts come from the complaints API. We store the count and the component names, not the complaint stories. A complaint is an owner report. It is not a finding that a part is defective. The VIN lookup at nhtsa.gov/recalls is the check for a specific car.

What we leave out

We do not print repair steps. We do not turn a Motorcraft part number into an inch length unless the manual prints the inches. We do not use another site's bulb chart as the source.

Dates

The published date is the day the page was first written. It does not change on a rebuild. The modified date changes when a fact on the page changes.

Which Car Fit: key fob battery, wiper parts, bulb sizes, and NHTSA recalls by make, model, and year. Independent, and not affiliated with any car maker. Confirm every part on the vehicle and in the owner's manual.
