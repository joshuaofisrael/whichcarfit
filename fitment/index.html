Which part fits my car?

Three fitment facts owners look up, and where each one is printed. This site copies the figure only when an owner's manual or a manufacturer parts table prints it.

Key fob battery

The owner's manual names the coin cell in the remote chapter. Some vehicles print two cells because the integrated key and the push button remote are different. Match the remote in your hand, not just the model year. See every page we have checked on the key fob battery hub.

Wiper size

Some manuals print an inch length. Ford's books often print a Motorcraft part number instead, and they do not print the inches next to it. We do not turn a part number into a length. Rear wipers are listed only when the manual prints a rear part. See the wiper blade hub.

Bulb chart

The bulb chart is in the owner's manual, usually near the capacities or the light bulb section. It is not on the tire placard. The tire placard, on the driver's door jamb, is for tire size and cold pressure. Where a chart lists halogen and LED rows, both are on the page, because the trim decides which one you have. See headlights, brake lights, and turn signals.

Recalls

Open recalls are looked up with the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. The year pages also list the campaigns NHTSA's public API returned for that model year.

Frequently asked questions

Which key fob battery fits my car?

Open the year page for your make, model, and year, or use the picker on the home page. The cell is whatever that year's owner's manual prints, often a CR2032, and sometimes a second cell for a different remote. Do not buy from the model name alone.

Which wiper blade fits my car?

Use the year page. If the manual prints an inch length, that length is on the page. If it prints only a part number, or does not print a length, the page says so. A length from another year is not a substitute.

Which bulb fits my car?

Use the year page for the headlight, brake light, and turn signal rows. The tire label on the driver's door jamb does not list bulb numbers. Match the row to your trim when the manual lists more than one lamp package.

Where is the key fob battery printed?

In the owner's manual chapter on the remote or the key. The cell type is a CR number such as CR2032. If the book lists two remotes, they can take two different cells.

Where do I look up a recall?

Enter the VIN at https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls. A campaign number on our year pages links to NHTSA's record for that campaign.
