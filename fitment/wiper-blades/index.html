Wiper blades by vehicle

Front and rear wiper parts printed in the owner's manual. Open the year page for the full chart and the manual link. A figure from one year is not a figure for the next year.

  • 2020 Ford F-150: Front wiper: Motorcraft part WW-2248 for the windshield wiper blade, printed in the parts tables. The manual does not print an inch length. Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed in those parts tables.
  • 2022 Ford F-150: Front wiper: Motorcraft part WW-2208 for the windshield wiper blade. No inch length is printed. Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed in the parts tables we read.
  • 2020 Ford Escape: Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2502 on the driver side and WW-2051 on the passenger side. No inch length is printed. Rear wiper: Motorcraft WW-1112 for the rear window.

  • 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility: Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2616 driver side and WW-2113 passenger side. No inch length is printed. Rear wiper: Motorcraft WW-1110 for the rear window.
