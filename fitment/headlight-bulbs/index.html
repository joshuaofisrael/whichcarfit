Headlight bulbs by vehicle

Low beam, high beam, and LED headlamp notes from the manual. Open the year page for the full chart and the manual link. A figure from one year is not a figure for the next year.

  • 2018 Ford F-150: Headlight: Low beam H11, 55 W. High beam 9005, 65 W. Daytime running lamps are LED.
  • 2020 Ford F-150: Headlight: Low beam H11, 55 W. High beam 9005, 65 W. A separate row lists headlamps as LED. Daytime running lamps are LED.
  • 2022 Ford F-150: Headlight: Headlamp low beam on XL and some XLT trucks: H11, 55 W. High beam on those trucks: 9005, 65 W. Headlamps on Lariat, Platinum, King Ranch, Limited, and some XLT trucks: LED. Daytime running lamp is H11, 55...
  • 2020 Ford Escape: Headlight: Base headlamp low beam H9. Base headlamp high beam HB3. Optional headlamp is LED, and the chart says to see a dealer for that lamp.
  • 2022 Ford Escape: Headlight: Base headlamp low beam H9. Base headlamp high beam HB3. Optional headlamp is LED. Base fog lamp H8. Optional fog lamp is LED.
  • 2023 Ford Ranger: Headlight: Low beam H11LL, 55 W. High beam HB3LL, 60 W. A separate row lists headlamps as LED. Front fog lamp bulb H11, 55 W, and a separate row lists front fog lamps as LED.
  • 2020 Ford Fusion: Headlight: High beam H9 if equipped, and a separate high beam row is LED. Low beam H11LL if equipped, and a separate low beam row is LED. Front fog lamp H8.
  • 2020 Ford Mustang: Headlight: The changing a bulb chapter says headlamps are LED and are not serviceable. It lists the same for the rear lamp, brake lamp, front fog lamp, front side marker, daytime running lamp, and central high...
  • 2020 Ford Expedition: Headlight: Halogen low beam H11, 55 W. Halogen high beam HB3, 55 W. A separate row lists the LED headlamp. Halogen front fog lamp 9140, 55 W, and a separate LED fog lamp row.
  • 2019 Ford Edge: Headlight: Low beam H11LL, 55 W. High beam 9005LL, 55 W. High intensity discharge headlamps: D3S. Front signature lamp: LED.
  • 2020 Ford Edge: Headlight: Low beam H11LL, 55 W. High beam 9005LL, 55 W. High intensity discharge headlamps: D3S. Front signature lamp: LED.
  • 2020 Lincoln Nautilus: Headlight: The bulb chart says all exterior lamps are LED and are not serviceable. See a dealer if one fails.
  • 2021 Ford E-Series: Headlight: Low beam and high beam are both listed as H13.
  • 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility: Headlight: Low beam and high beam are both LED.
  • 2020 Jeep Wrangler: Headlight: Headlamps: H13. Premium head lamps: LED, serviced at a dealer.
  • 2024 Jeep Wrangler: Headlight: Headlamps: H13. Premium head lamps: LED.
  • 2026 Jeep Wrangler: Headlight: Headlamps: H13. Premium head lamps: LED. The manual says to see a dealer for LED replacement.
  • 2023 Jeep Cherokee: Headlight: Low beam and high beam headlamps: LED.
  • 2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee: Headlight: The manual says the exterior lights are LED. See a dealer for replacement.
  • 2020 Ram 1500: Headlight: Halogen reflector low beam: H11LL. Halogen reflector high beam: 9005LL. LED reflector and LED projector headlamps: LED, serviced at a dealer.
  • 2023 Ram 1500: Headlight: Halogen reflector low beam: H11LL. Halogen reflector high beam: 9005LL. LED reflector and LED projector headlamps: LED.
  • 2026 Ram 1500: Headlight: The exterior bulbs chapter says to see a dealer for service on LED lamps, including the LED reflector headlamp and LED front fog lamps. It does not print an H11 or 9005 number in the section we read.
  • 2023 Dodge Durango: Headlight: Low beam, high beam headlamps: LED.
  • 2018 Honda Civic: Headlight: Low beam: 55 W (H11) on halogen bulb type models, or LED. High beam: 60 W (HB3) on halogen models, or LED. The specifications page uses footnotes for halogen headlights and LED headlights.
  • 2025 Honda CR-V: Headlight: Headlights are LED. The manual says to have a dealer inspect and replace the light assembly.
  • 2024 Toyota Grand Highlander: Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the specifications light bulb table. That table only lists a replaceable bulb for the front turn and parking lights.
  • 2025 Toyota Corolla Hybrid: Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the specifications light bulb table.
  • 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid: Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the specifications light bulb table.
