Which Corolla key and bulb fits?

This hub is the gas Corolla sedan. It is not the Corolla Hybrid and not the Corolla Cross Hybrid. Checked 21 Nov 2026.

Three gas books print one key and one exterior table, so they are one page. 2020, OM02580U, 2021, OM02582U, and 2022, OM02617U. Helm sells OM02617U as the 2022 Corolla owner's manual. The key is a CR2032. The light bulb table prints front side marker 5 W with no trade number, front turn and parking 7444NA at 28/8 W on cars without side turn signals, rear side marker (bulb type) 5 W, rear turn 21 W, and back-up 16 W. Headlights, daytime running lights, stop lights, and tail lights are on the dealer list. None of the three books prints a wiper inch length. That chart is on the 2020 Corolla page.

The 2023 book OM02568U (software identifier corolla.2023.2304.cv.vh) and the 2024 book OM02684U (corolla.2024.2309.cv.vh) split the key: CR2032 without a smart key system, CR2450 with one. The wattage numbers match the earlier table. The dealer list adds front side marker lights (LED type). An earlier 2023 printing, OM02525U, prints the same split and the same table. That chart is on the 2023 Corolla page.

A 2025 gas Corolla owner's manual was not in the publication set we opened. Do not use the 2025 Corolla Hybrid book for a gas sedan. That hybrid book prints CR2032 and does not print the 7444NA row. NHTSA's recalls API, retrieved 21 Nov 2026, returned 3 campaigns for 2020, 1 for 2021, none for 2022, 3 for 2023, 2 for 2024, and none for 2025. The 2022 and 2025 responses were HTTP 400 with Count 0 and an empty results list.

Which part fits this Corolla?

Which key fob battery fits a gas Toyota Corolla?

2020, 2021, and 2022 gas books print CR2032 only. The 2023 and 2024 gas books print CR2032 without a smart key system and CR2450 with one. The 2025 Corolla Hybrid book is a different car and prints CR2032.

Which wiper blade fits a gas Toyota Corolla?

The 2020 through 2024 gas books we opened do not print a driver or passenger inch length. Do not borrow a length from a parts listing.

Which bulb fits a gas Toyota Corolla?

The exterior table prints front turn and parking 7444NA at 28/8 W, rear turn 21 W with no trade number, and back-up 16 W with no trade number. Headlights and stop lights are on the dealer list. From 2023 the dealer list also names LED front side marker lights.
