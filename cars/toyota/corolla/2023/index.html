2023 Corolla key is CR2032 or CR2450

Owner's manual checked 21 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: CR2032 on vehicles without a smart key system, or CR2450 on vehicles with a smart key system. The 2020 to 2022 gas books print CR2032 only.
  • Front wiper: The maintenance chapter has a wiper insert procedure. The 2023 and 2024 books do not print an inch length.
  • Rear wiper: This sedan manual does not print a rear wiper length.
  • Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the light bulb table. Headlights and daytime running lights are on the list of bulbs your Toyota dealer replaces.
  • Brake light: Stop lights, tail lights, and the high mounted stoplight are on the dealer list. No stop lamp trade number is printed.
  • Turn signal: Front side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. Front turn signal and parking lights, vehicles without side turn signal lights: 7444NA, 28/8 W, wedge base amber. Rear side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. Rear turn signal lights: 21 W, wedge base amber, no trade number. Back-up lights: 16 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. The dealer list adds front side marker lights (LED type), which the 2020 to 2022 dealer list does not.

Gas sedan books only. The software identifiers say corolla.2023 and corolla.2024, not the hybrid file. This page is the 2023 chart and the 2024 chart. Recalls below are model year 2023 only. NHTSA's recalls API, retrieved 21 Nov 2026, returned 2 campaigns for 2024. A 2025 gas Corolla owner's manual was not on the publication list we opened. The Corolla Hybrid 2025 book is a different car.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2023 Toyota Corolla key fob take?

CR2032 on vehicles without a smart key system, or CR2450 on vehicles with a smart key system. The 2020 to 2022 gas books print CR2032 only. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2023 Toyota Corolla?

Battery: CR2032 on vehicles without a smart key system, or CR2450 on vehicles with a smart key system. The 2020 to 2022 gas books print CR2032 only. Front wiper: The maintenance chapter has a wiper insert procedure. The 2023 and 2024 books do not print an inch length. Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the light bulb table. Headlights and daytime running lights are on the list of bulbs your Toyota dealer replaces. Brake light: Stop lights, tail lights, and the high mounted stoplight are on the dealer list. No stop lamp trade number is printed. Turn signal: Front side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. Front turn signal and parking lights, vehicles without side turn signal lights: 7444NA, 28/8 W, wedge base amber. Rear side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. Rear turn signal lights: 21 W, wedge base amber, no trade number. Back-up lights: 16 W, wedge base clear, no trade number. The dealer list adds front side marker lights (LED type), which the 2020 to 2022 dealer list does not.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2023 Toyota Corolla?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2023 Toyota Corolla?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 21 Nov 2026.
