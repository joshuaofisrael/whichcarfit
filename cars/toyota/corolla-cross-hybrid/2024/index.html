Which parts fit a 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Lithium battery CR2450.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the pages we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the pages we read.
  • Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the specifications light bulb table.
  • Brake light: Tail lights and rear side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W. The table prints a dash instead of a bulb number.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal, bulb type: 21 W, wedge base amber, no bulb number printed. Front side marker and parking lights, bulb type: 5 W each, no bulb number printed. Back up lights: 16 W. License plate lights: 5 W.

This is the hybrid book. The software identifier says corollacross.2024. Confirm it matches the door label.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid key fob take?

Lithium battery CR2450. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid?

Battery: Lithium battery CR2450. Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the pages we read. Headlight: A headlight trade number is not in the specifications light bulb table. Brake light: Tail lights and rear side marker lights, bulb type: 5 W. The table prints a dash instead of a bulb number. Turn signal: Front turn signal, bulb type: 21 W, wedge base amber, no bulb number printed. Front side marker and parking lights, bulb type: 5 W each, no bulb number printed. Back up lights: 16 W. License plate lights: 5 W.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2024 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
