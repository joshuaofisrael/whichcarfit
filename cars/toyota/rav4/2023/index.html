Which parts fit a 2023 Toyota RAV4?

Owner's manual checked 19 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Lithium battery CR2032 for the wireless remote control or electronic key.
  • Front wiper: The manual has a wiper insert replacement section. It does not print an inch length in the pages we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the pages we read.
  • Headlight: Headlights, daytime running lights, LED type front turn signals, LED type parking lights, LED type front side marker lights, fog lights (if equipped), tail lights, stop lights, rear side marker lights, the high mounted stoplight, and license plate lights are listed among the lamps your Toyota dealer replaces. No headlight trade number is printed in the light bulb table.
  • Brake light: Stop lights, tail lights, rear side marker lights, and the high mounted stoplight are listed among the lamps your Toyota dealer replaces. No stop lamp trade number is printed in the light bulb table.
  • Turn signal: Front side marker lights (bulb type): W5W, 5 W, wedge base clear. Front turn signal and parking lights (type A): 7444NA, 28/8 W, wedge base amber. Front turn signal (type B and C, bulb type): 7444NA, 28/8 W (only the 28 W filament illuminates). Rear turn signal lights: WY21W, 21 W, wedge base amber. Back-up lights: W16W, 16 W, wedge base clear. Fog lights (if equipped) are on the dealer replacement list, not in the DIY bulb table.

Gas RAV4 book only, not Hybrid or Prime. From this year the fog lamp row leaves the DIY light bulb table and joins the dealer LED list. Match type A, B, or C front lamp packages to the vehicle.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2023 Toyota RAV4 key fob take?

Lithium battery CR2032 for the wireless remote control or electronic key. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Battery: Lithium battery CR2032 for the wireless remote control or electronic key. Front wiper: The manual has a wiper insert replacement section. It does not print an inch length in the pages we read. Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2023 Toyota RAV4?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2023 Toyota RAV4?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 19 Nov 2026.
