2025 Camry key fob battery is CR2450

Owner's manual checked 20 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Lithium battery CR2450. The 2019 to 2024 Camry books print CR2032 instead.
  • Front wiper: The manual discusses wiper service position and wiper inserts. It does not print an inch length.
  • Rear wiper: This sedan manual does not print a rear wiper length.
  • Headlight: Headlights are not in the light bulb table. The maintenance chapter says lamps other than the three bulb-type positions below are LEDs, replaced by a Toyota dealer.
  • Brake light: Stop lights are in the LED list, not in the bulb table. No stop lamp trade number is printed.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal and parking lights, bulb type: 7444NA, 28/8 W, wedge base amber. Rear turn signal lights, bulb type: WY21W, 21 W, wedge base amber. Back-up lights, bulb type: W16W, 16 W, wedge base clear. There is no W5W side marker row and no 921 row, unlike the 2019 to 2024 table.

NHTSA files this year under CAMRY HYBRID, and that is the model name used for the recall list. We did not find a separate 2025 gas Camry owner's manual on Toyota's manual host. The 2019 to 2024 gas books are the CR2032 and 921 chart on the 2023 page.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2025 Toyota Camry key fob take?

Lithium battery CR2450. The 2019 to 2024 Camry books print CR2032 instead. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2025 Toyota Camry?

Battery: Lithium battery CR2450. The 2019 to 2024 Camry books print CR2032 instead. Front wiper: The manual discusses wiper service position and wiper inserts. It does not print an inch length. Headlight: Headlights are not in the light bulb table. The maintenance chapter says lamps other than the three bulb-type positions below are LEDs, replaced by a Toyota dealer. Brake light: Stop lights are in the LED list, not in the bulb table. No stop lamp trade number is printed. Turn signal: Front turn signal and parking lights, bulb type: 7444NA, 28/8 W, wedge base amber. Rear turn signal lights, bulb type: WY21W, 21 W, wedge base amber. Back-up lights, bulb type: W16W, 16 W, wedge base clear. There is no W5W side marker row and no 921 row, unlike the 2019 to 2024 table.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2025 Toyota Camry?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2025 Toyota Camry?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 20 Nov 2026.
