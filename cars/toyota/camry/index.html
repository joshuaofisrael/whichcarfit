Which Toyota Camry year should I pick?

The 2019 through 2024 Camry books we opened on 20 Nov 2026 print one shared exterior bulb chart and a CR2032 key. Publishing six copies of that chart would be the same page with the year changed, so those years live on the 2023 Camry page. The 2025 book is a different car and has its own page.

Books that match the 2023 chart: 2019 OM06142U (light bulb table page 550), 2020 OM06158U (page 558), 2021 OM06242U (page 599), 2022 OM06244U (page 589), 2023 OM06259U (page 589), and 2024 OM06291U (page 589). Each table lists front side marker W5W 5 W, front turn and parking 7444NA 28/8 W, rear turn WY21W 21 W, rear side marker W5W 5 W, and back-up 921 16 W. The footnote says bulbs not listed are LED. Each key chapter says lithium battery CR2032. None of these books prints a wiper inch length.

Two lines next to that chart do move, and neither one is a lamp you buy from the bulb table. The 2019 and 2020 specifications chapter lists a 19 x 8 J wheel. From 2021 the same line reads 19 x 8 1/2 J. The 2019, 2020, and 2021 tables still include a vanity light row. The 2022, 2023, and 2024 tables do not.

The 2025 file is the Camry Hybrid owner's manual, publication OM06266U, software identifier camry.2025.2403.hev.vh. The key is a CR2450, not a CR2032. The light bulb table on page 492 lists front turn and parking 7444NA 28/8 W, rear turn WY21W 21 W, and back-up W16W 16 W. There is no W5W row and no 921 row. The maintenance chapter says lamps other than those three bulb-type positions are LEDs, and a Toyota dealer replaces those. NHTSA files this year as CAMRY HYBRID, not as CAMRY.

Which part fits this Camry?

Which key fob battery fits a Toyota Camry?

It depends on the year, and sometimes on which remote is in the car. Open the year page. The short battery line for each year we have checked is in the table on this page.

Which wiper blade fits a Toyota Camry?

Use the year page. Some manuals print an inch length, and some print only a part number or say the length is not in the book. Do not reuse a length from a different year.

Which bulb fits a Toyota Camry?

The headlight, brake, and turn rows are on the year page, copied from that year's owner's manual. Trims in the same year can take different lamps.
