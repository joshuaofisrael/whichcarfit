2020 Ram 1500 key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: One CR2450.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper length is printed. This is a pickup.
  • Headlight: Halogen reflector low beam: H11LL. Halogen reflector high beam: 9005LL. LED reflector and LED projector headlamps: LED, serviced at a dealer.
  • Brake light: Base rear tail, turn and stop lamp: 7440LL or W21WLL. Premium rear lamp: LED. Center high mounted stop lamp: 921.
  • Turn signal: Turn signal and front position on the halogen reflector headlamp: 7444NA. The LED headlamp version of that lamp is dealer serviced. Base turn lamp: 7440NA or WY21W. Front fog lamps on the halogen reflector headlamp: H11LL.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2020 Ram 1500 key fob take?

One CR2450. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2020 Ram 1500?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2020 Ram 1500?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
