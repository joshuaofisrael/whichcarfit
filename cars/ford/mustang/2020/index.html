Which parts fit a 2020 Ford Mustang?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: One CR2450, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2160 driver side and WW-1964 passenger side. No inch length is printed.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed. This is a coupe and convertible manual.
  • Headlight: The changing a bulb chapter says headlamps are LED and are not serviceable. It lists the same for the rear lamp, brake lamp, front fog lamp, front side marker, daytime running lamp, and central high mounted brake lamp.
  • Brake light: Listed with the LED lamps that are not serviceable. See a dealer if one fails.
  • Turn signal: Not given a separate trade number in the LED list we read. The reverse lamp is described as a bulb you can rotate out, but that procedure does not print a trade number.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2020 Ford Mustang key fob take?

One CR2450, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2020 Ford Mustang?

Battery: One CR2450, or equivalent. Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2160 driver side and WW-1964 passenger side. No inch length is printed. Headlight: The changing a bulb chapter says headlamps are LED and are not serviceable. It lists the same for the rear lamp, brake lamp, front fog lamp, front side marker, daytime running lamp, and central high mounted brake lamp. Brake light: Listed with the LED lamps that are not serviceable. See a dealer if one fails. Turn signal: Not given a separate trade number in the LED list we read. The reverse lamp is described as a bulb you can rotate out, but that procedure does not print a trade number.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2020 Ford Mustang?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2020 Ford Mustang?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
