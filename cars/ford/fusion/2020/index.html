2020 Ford Fusion key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: A coin cell type was not printed in the passages we searched. Open the remote battery steps in this edition before you buy a cell.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2601 driver side and WW-2700 passenger side. No inch length is printed.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed next to those windshield parts.
  • Headlight: High beam H9 if equipped, and a separate high beam row is LED. Low beam H11LL if equipped, and a separate low beam row is LED. Front fog lamp H8.
  • Brake light: Tail and brake lamp, high series and low series: both rows are LED.
  • Turn signal: Front direction indicator: PWY24W. Front side marker lamp: LED. Reverse lamp: 921.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2020 Ford Fusion key fob take?

A coin cell type was not printed in the passages we searched. Open the remote battery steps in this edition before you buy a cell. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2020 Ford Fusion?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2020 Ford Fusion?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
