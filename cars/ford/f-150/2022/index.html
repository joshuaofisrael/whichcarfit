Which parts fit a 2022 Ford F-150?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: One coin type 3 volt lithium battery. The manual prints CR2032 for one remote and CR2450 for the other.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft part WW-2208 for the windshield wiper blade. No inch length is printed.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed in the parts tables we read.
  • Headlight: Headlamp low beam on XL and some XLT trucks: H11, 55 W. High beam on those trucks: 9005, 65 W. Headlamps on Lariat, Platinum, King Ranch, Limited, and some XLT trucks: LED. Daytime running lamp is H11, 55 W, on XL and some XLT trucks, and LED on the higher trims.
  • Brake light: Central high mounted stop lamp: 912, 16 W. Taillamp, all functions, on Lariat and higher trims: LED. Rear lamp, stop lamp, rear turn signal and rear side marker on XL optional and XLT: WT21W/7W, 21/7 W.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal and parking lamp: 4257NA, 21/5 W, on XL and some XLT trucks, and LED on higher trims. Rear turn on XL optional: WTY21W, 21 W. Side turn signal lamp: LED.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2022 Ford F-150 key fob take?

One coin type 3 volt lithium battery. The manual prints CR2032 for one remote and CR2450 for the other. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2022 Ford F-150?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2022 Ford F-150?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
