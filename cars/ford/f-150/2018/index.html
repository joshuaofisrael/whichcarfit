2018 Ford F-150 key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Integrated keyhead transmitter: one CR2032, or equivalent. Intelligent Access key: one CR2450, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft parts tables print windshield wiper blade WW-2242 and, in another table, WW-2247. The manual does not print an inch length or say which table is which cab.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper part is printed in the Motorcraft parts tables we read.
  • Headlight: Low beam H11, 55 W. High beam 9005, 65 W. Daytime running lamps are LED.
  • Brake light: Rear lamps, brake lamps, rear direction indicator and rear side marker lamps: 3157, 27/8 W. The chart says these may have optional LED lamps, which are not serviceable. Central high mounted brake lamp: 921, 16 W, or LED on vehicles with a cargo box lamp.
  • Turn signal: Front direction indicator and parking lamps: 7444NA, 21/5 W. Side direction indicator: LED. Rear direction indicator is included in the 3157 rear lamp above.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2018 Ford F-150 key fob take?

Integrated keyhead transmitter: one CR2032, or equivalent. Intelligent Access key: one CR2450, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2018 Ford F-150?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2018 Ford F-150?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
