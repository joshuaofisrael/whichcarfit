2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: One CR2032, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2616 driver side and WW-2113 passenger side. No inch length is printed.
  • Rear wiper: Motorcraft WW-1110 for the rear window.
  • Headlight: Low beam and high beam are both LED.
  • Brake light: Brake and rear lamp: LED. Central high mounted brake lamp: LED.
  • Turn signal: Front direction indicator: LED. Rear direction indicator: 3757NAK or PY27/7W, 27 W. Reversing lamp: W21W, 21 W. License plate lamp: W5W, 5 W.

NHTSA's recalls API does not accept Police Interceptor Utility as a model name. This page does not borrow civilian Explorer recalls. Look up the VIN.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility key fob take?

One CR2032, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility?

Battery: One CR2032, or equivalent. Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2616 driver side and WW-2113 passenger side. No inch length is printed. Headlight: Low beam and high beam are both LED. Brake light: Brake and rear lamp: LED. Central high mounted brake lamp: LED. Turn signal: Front direction indicator: LED. Rear direction indicator: 3757NAK or PY27/7W, 27 W. Reversing lamp: W21W, 21 W. License plate lamp: W5W, 5 W.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2021 Ford Police Interceptor Utility?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
