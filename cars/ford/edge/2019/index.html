Which parts fit a 2019 Ford Edge?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: A coin cell type was not printed in the passages we searched.
  • Front wiper: Motorcraft WW-2755 driver side and WW-2760 passenger side. No inch length is printed.
  • Rear wiper: Motorcraft WW-1414 for the rear window.
  • Headlight: Low beam H11LL, 55 W. High beam 9005LL, 55 W. High intensity discharge headlamps: D3S. Front signature lamp: LED.
  • Brake light: The rows we read do not give a separate brake trade number. Reversing lamps are LED.
  • Turn signal: Rear direction indicator: LED. Side direction indicator: LED. License plate lamp: W5W, 5 W.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2019 Ford Edge?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2019 Ford Edge?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
