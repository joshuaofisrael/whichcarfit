2020 and 2022 CR-V: H11 or LED

Owner's manual checked 21 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Master keys with a remote transmitter: CR1620. Keyless remote: CR2032. The 2022 book prints the same two cells. The 2023 to 2025 books print only the CR2032.
  • Front wiper: Checking and Maintaining Wiper Blades describes replacing the rubber. The 2020 book and the 2022 book do not print a driver or passenger inch length.
  • Rear wiper: The rear-wiper section describes changing the rubber. Neither the 2020 book nor the 2022 book prints an inch length.
  • Headlight: Low beam: 55 W (H11) on halogen bulb type models, or LED. High beam: 60 W (HB3) on halogen bulb type models, or LED. The book says LED headlights are replaced as an assembly by a dealer.
  • Brake light: Brake, taillight, rear turn signal, and rear side marker lights are LED. High-mount brake light is LED. Have a dealer replace those assemblies. Back-up light: 16 W.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal and front side marker lights: LED. Parking and daytime running lights: LED. Rear turn signal is LED, in the brake and taillight assembly, not a separate 21 W bulb. Side turn signals on the door mirrors: LED.

This page is the 2020 book and the 2022 book. They print the same chart, so 2022 is not a second page. A 2021 CR-V owner's manual was not opened on 21 Nov 2026, and this chart is not copied onto 2021. The 2023 to 2025 chart is different and lives on the 2025 page. Recalls below are model year 2020 only. NHTSA's recalls API, retrieved 21 Nov 2026, returned 4 campaigns for 2021 and 3 for 2022. Look up the VIN.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2020 Honda CR-V key fob take?

Master keys with a remote transmitter: CR1620. Keyless remote: CR2032. The 2022 book prints the same two cells. The 2023 to 2025 books print only the CR2032. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Battery: Master keys with a remote transmitter: CR1620. Keyless remote: CR2032. The 2022 book prints the same two cells. The 2023 to 2025 books print only the CR2032. Brake light: Brake, taillight, rear turn signal, and rear side marker lights are LED. High-mount brake light is LED. Have a dealer replace those assemblies. Back-up light: 16 W. Turn signal: Front turn signal and front side marker lights: LED. Parking and daytime running lights: LED. Rear turn signal is LED, in the brake and taillight assembly, not a separate 21 W bulb. Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2020 Honda CR-V?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2020 Honda CR-V?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 21 Nov 2026.
