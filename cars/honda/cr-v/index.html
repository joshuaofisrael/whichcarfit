Which CR-V bulb and key fob fits?

Two CR-V charts are on this site, and they are not the same car. Checked 21 Nov 2026.

The 2020 book (ATLA2020OM) and the 2022 book print a halogen low beam of 55 W (H11) and a halogen high beam of 60 W (HB3), and they also list LED headlights that a dealer replaces as an assembly. Front turn signals are LED. Brake, taillight, rear turn, and rear side marker lights are LED. The back-up light is 16 W. Remote Transmitter Care prints CR1620 for a master key with a remote, and CR2032 for the keyless remote. Neither wiper chapter prints an inch length. Those two years live on the 2020 CR-V page. A 2021 CR-V owner's manual was not opened, so 2021 is not given either chart.

The 2023, 2024, and 2025 books drop the H11 and HB3 rows. Headlights are LED. Back-up lights are LED. The rear turn signal is 21 W amber. The keyless remote is a CR2032, and those books do not print a CR1620 cell. The 2023 and 2025 wiper chapters still do not print an inch length. The 2024 specifications page and the 2024 remote-battery page match this chart. The 2024 wiper chapter was not the page we opened. That chart is on the 2025 CR-V page.

NHTSA's recalls API, retrieved 21 Nov 2026, returned 7 campaigns for 2020, 4 for 2021, 3 for 2022, 3 for 2023, 2 for 2024, and 1 for 2025. A campaign count is not a reason to buy a bulb. Use the VIN lookup. Nearby charts: Accord, Civic, and the RAV4.

Which part fits this CR-V?

Which key fob battery fits a Honda CR-V?

The 2020 and 2022 books print CR1620 for a master key with a remote transmitter and CR2032 for the keyless remote. The 2023, 2024, and 2025 books print CR2032 only. A 2021 book was not opened.

Which wiper blade fits a Honda CR-V?

None of the CR-V books we opened prints a driver, passenger, or rear inch length. The 2024 wiper chapter was not the page we opened. Do not borrow a length from another year.

Which bulb fits a Honda CR-V?

2020 and 2022: halogen low beam H11 55 W or LED, halogen high beam HB3 60 W or LED, back-up light 16 W, rear turn LED. 2023 to 2025: headlights LED, back-up lights LED, rear turn 21 W amber.
