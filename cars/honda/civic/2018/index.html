Which parts fit a 2018 Honda Civic?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Master keys with a remote transmitter: CR1620. Smart entry remote: CR2032. The manual marks each one as not available on all models. Page 562.
  • Front wiper: Checking and Maintaining Wiper Blades, about pages 547 to 549, tells you how to change the blade rubber. It does not print a driver or passenger inch length.
  • Rear wiper: This sedan manual does not print a rear wiper length.
  • Headlight: Low beam: 55 W (H11) on halogen bulb type models, or LED. High beam: 60 W (HB3) on halogen models, or LED. The specifications page uses footnotes for halogen headlights and LED headlights.
  • Brake light: Brake lights: 21 W. High mount brake light: 21 W on models without sport mode, or LED on models with sport mode, according to the footnotes on the specifications page. Tail and rear side marker lights: LED.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal lights: 21 W amber on halogen headlight models, or LED on LED headlight models. Rear turn signal lights: 21 W amber. Back up lights: 16 W.

Figures are for the 2018 Civic sedan book. A hatchback or coupe can differ. The manual says some items are not on all models.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2018 Honda Civic key fob take?

Master keys with a remote transmitter: CR1620. Smart entry remote: CR2032. The manual marks each one as not available on all models. Page 562. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2018 Honda Civic?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2018 Honda Civic?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
