Which parts fit a 2023 Honda Accord?

Owner's manual checked 18 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: Remote Transmitter Care, Replacing the Remote Battery: battery type CR2032.
  • Front wiper: Checking and Maintaining Wiper Blades gives the rubber-replacement steps. It does not print a driver or passenger inch length.
  • Rear wiper: This sedan manual does not print a rear wiper length.
  • Headlight: Headlights are LED type. The manual says to have an authorized Honda dealer inspect and replace the light assembly.
  • Brake light: Brake lights, rear side marker and taillights, and rear turn signal lights are LED type, replaced as an assembly by a dealer.
  • Turn signal: Front turn signal lights and front side marker lights are LED type. Rear turn signal lights are LED type, grouped with the brake and taillight assembly. The specifications table lists every exterior light bulb position as LED, with no bulb trade number on this model.

Every exterior lamp on this model is LED and replaced at a dealer as an assembly. That is what the manual's own specifications table says, not a fact we could not find.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2023 Honda Accord key fob take?

Remote Transmitter Care, Replacing the Remote Battery: battery type CR2032. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2023 Honda Accord?

Battery: Remote Transmitter Care, Replacing the Remote Battery: battery type CR2032. Front wiper: Checking and Maintaining Wiper Blades gives the rubber-replacement steps. It does not print a driver or passenger inch length. Headlight: Headlights are LED type. The manual says to have an authorized Honda dealer inspect and replace the light assembly. Brake light: Brake lights, rear side marker and taillights, and rear turn signal lights are LED type, replaced as an assembly by a dealer. Turn signal: Front turn signal lights and front side marker lights are LED type. Rear turn signal lights are LED type, grouped with the brake and taillight assembly. The specifications table lists every exterior light bulb position as LED, with no bulb trade number on this model.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2023 Honda Accord?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2023 Honda Accord?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 18 Nov 2026.
