2022 Nissan Altima key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Headlight: A headlight trade number was not in the rows we read.
  • Brake light: Tail: W5W.
  • Turn signal: Turn signal/park (if so equipped) light: 7444NA. Turn: W21W.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2022 Nissan Altima key fob take?

The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2022 Nissan Altima?

Battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read. Headlight: A headlight trade number was not in the rows we read. Brake light: Tail: W5W. Turn signal: Turn signal/park (if so equipped) light: 7444NA. Turn: W21W.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2022 Nissan Altima?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2022 Nissan Altima?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
