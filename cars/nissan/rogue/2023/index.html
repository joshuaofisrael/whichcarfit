Which parts fit a 2023 Nissan Rogue?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Headlight: The manual says to visit a Nissan dealer if an LED headlight needs replacement. The light chart marks the high and low beams as LED.
  • Brake light: Stop and tail lamps on the chart we read are marked LED where a separate bulb is not listed.
  • Turn signal: The exterior light chart lists the front turn signal on its own row. Some positions on that chart are LED and some print a trade number, so match the row before you buy a bulb.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart. See the fitment guide for how we read a chart, and the key fob battery hub if you only need the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2023 Nissan Rogue key fob take?

The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2023 Nissan Rogue?

Battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read. Headlight: The manual says to visit a Nissan dealer if an LED headlight needs replacement. The light chart marks the high and low beams as LED. Brake light: Stop and tail lamps on the chart we read are marked LED where a separate bulb is not listed. Turn signal: The exterior light chart lists the front turn signal on its own row. Some positions on that chart are LED and some print a trade number, so match the row before you buy a bulb.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2023 Nissan Rogue?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2023 Nissan Rogue?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
