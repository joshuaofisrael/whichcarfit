Which parts fit a 2024 Jeep Wrangler?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: The recommended replacement battery is CR2450.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Headlight: Headlamps: H13. Premium head lamps: LED.
  • Brake light: Rear base tail lamp stop, tail and turn bulb: 3157. Center high mounted stop lamp: LED. Premium LED tail lamps: LED.
  • Turn signal: Sport front park and turn: 7444NALL. Premium front park and turn: LED. Base Sahara and Rubicon turn lamp: 7440NALL or WY21WLL. Base park and daytime running lamp: 7443LL. Front side marker: LED. Base fog lamps: PSX24W. Premium fog lamps: LED.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2024 Jeep Wrangler key fob take?

The recommended replacement battery is CR2450. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2024 Jeep Wrangler?

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2024 Jeep Wrangler?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2024 Jeep Wrangler?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
