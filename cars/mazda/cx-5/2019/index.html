2019 Mazda CX-5 key fob battery, wiper size, bulb size, and common problems

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: The key transmitter battery is a CR2025, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Rear wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read.
  • Headlight: The maintenance chapter says the exterior lights have either LEDs or normal bulbs. Headlights are listed with the LED lamps, which the manual says are replaced as a unit. The front turn signal procedure does not print a trade number.
  • Brake light: Brake lights are listed with the LED lamps on at least one type. The section we read does not print a separate trade number for every brake lamp.
  • Turn signal: The front turn signal and parking light procedure does not print a trade number.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2019 Mazda CX-5 key fob take?

The key transmitter battery is a CR2025, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2019 Mazda CX-5?

Battery: The key transmitter battery is a CR2025, or equivalent. Front wiper: An inch length is not printed in the wiper section we read. Headlight: The maintenance chapter says the exterior lights have either LEDs or normal bulbs. Headlights are listed with the LED lamps, which the manual says are replaced as a unit. The front turn signal procedure does not print a trade number. Brake light: Brake lights are listed with the LED lamps on at least one type. The section we read does not print a separate trade number for every brake lamp. Turn signal: The front turn signal and parking light procedure does not print a trade number.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2019 Mazda CX-5?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2019 Mazda CX-5?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
