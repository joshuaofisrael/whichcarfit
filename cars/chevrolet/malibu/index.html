Which Malibu wiper length fits?

The 2020 Malibu book was opened again on 21 Nov 2026. Document 13555849, CRC 8/16/19. The maintenance replacement parts table on page 314 prints a driver blade of 650 mm (25.6 in), GM part 23353586, and a passenger blade of 450 mm (17.7 in), GM part 23353587. There is no rear wiper line in that table. The remote battery line is still CR2032. The LED Lighting section says the vehicle has several LED lamps and to contact the dealer for an LED assembly. It does not print a halogen trade number.

The 2021 and 2022 pages already quote 25.6 in and 17.7 in from those years' books. Those two PDFs were not opened again today, so this paragraph does not add part numbers for them. The refreshed chart is on the 2020 Malibu page.

Which part fits this Malibu?

Which wiper blade fits a 2020 Chevrolet Malibu?

Driver side 650 mm (25.6 in), GM part 23353586. Passenger side 450 mm (17.7 in), GM part 23353587. The 2020 maintenance table does not print a rear wiper.

Which key fob battery fits a Chevrolet Malibu?

The 2020 book says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. The 2021 and 2022 pages quote the same cell from those years' books.

Which bulb fits a Chevrolet Malibu?

The 2020 LED Lighting section says the vehicle has several LED lamps and to contact the dealer for an LED assembly. It does not print a halogen trade number.
