Which Equinox wiper length fits?

The three Equinox books already on this site do not print one wiper table. Re-read of the 2022 book on 21 Nov 2026, with the 2020 and 2021 tables left as they were checked on 20 Nov 2026.

The 2020 book (document 13555863) prints 60 cm (23.6 in), 45.0 cm (17.7 in), and 30.0 cm (11.8 in), GM parts 84574893, 84574892, and 84215609. The 2021 book (document 14420010) keeps those centimeter lengths and those part numbers, and prints the inch side as 24, 18, and 12. The 2022 book (document 15230998, CRC 8/10/21) still prints 24, 18, and 12 inches. The driver part is now 84566977, not 84574893. The rear part is now 85118765, not 84215609. The passenger part stays 84574892.

All three books say to replace the remote battery with a CR2032. The 2022 headlamp chapter shows a bi-halogen bulb procedure and does not print a trade number. The same chapter says LED assemblies go to the dealer. Open the 2020, 2021, or 2022 page for the full line and that year's recalls. The wiper hub lists every length we have copied.

Which part fits this Equinox?

Which wiper blade fits a Chevrolet Equinox?

2020 prints 23.6, 17.7, and 11.8 inches with GM parts 84574893, 84574892, and 84215609. 2021 prints 24, 18, and 12 inches with the same part numbers. 2022 keeps 24, 18, and 12, changes the driver part to 84566977, and changes the rear part to 85118765.

Which key fob battery fits a Chevrolet Equinox?

The 2020, 2021, and 2022 books each say to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent.

Which headlight bulb fits a Chevrolet Equinox?

The 2022 book shows a bi-halogen headlamp procedure and does not print a trade number. It also says LED assemblies are replaced at the dealer. Do not guess an H11 from a parts counter.
