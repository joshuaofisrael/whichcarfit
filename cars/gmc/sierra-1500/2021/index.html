Which parts fit a 2021 GMC Sierra 1500?

Owner's manual checked 16 Nov 2026

Direct answer

  • Key fob battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent.
  • Front wiper: Driver side: 21.7 in. Passenger side: 21.7 in. Lengths are from the maintenance parts table.
  • Rear wiper: No rear wiper length is printed in the maintenance parts table.
  • Headlight: The bulb replacement chapter says this vehicle has several LED lamps and to contact a dealer for an LED assembly. The procedure we read does not print a halogen trade number.
  • Brake light: A brake lamp trade number is not printed in the section we read.
  • Turn signal: A turn signal trade number is not printed in the section we read.

Confirm every part against the owner's manual and the vehicle itself. This is a guide, not a repair instruction. Lamp packages change by trim, and a later book can change a chart.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does the 2021 GMC Sierra 1500 key fob take?

The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Match the remote you hold to the description, because some model years print two different cells.

Which wiper, bulb, and battery fit a 2021 GMC Sierra 1500?

Battery: The owner's manual says to replace the remote battery with a CR2032, or equivalent. Front wiper: Driver side: 21.7 in. Passenger side: 21.7 in. Lengths are from the maintenance parts table. Headlight: The bulb replacement chapter says this vehicle has several LED lamps and to contact a dealer for an LED assembly. The procedure we read does not print a halogen trade number. Brake light: A brake lamp trade number is not printed in the section we read. Turn signal: A turn signal trade number is not printed in the section we read.

Where do I find the tire size and the bulb chart for a 2021 GMC Sierra 1500?

Tire size and cold pressure are on the driver's door jamb label. The bulb types in the table on this page are taken from the owner's manual cited below, not from that label. If the manual lists more than one lamp package, match the row to the lamps on your vehicle.

Where do I look up recalls on a 2021 GMC Sierra 1500?

Go to https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter the VIN from the driver's door jamb. The campaigns listed on this page are what NHTSA's public recalls API returned for this model year on 16 Nov 2026.
