Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Car Fit (whichcarfit.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics on every page to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. We use Google Analytics 4 only after you accept the cookie banner. Consent Mode v2 keeps ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage denied until then, and the Google Analytics script does not load. If you accept, analytics storage is granted and Google Analytics 4 may set a cookie to measure visits. Accepting this banner does not grant ad storage. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice. Choosing reject after an accept sets analytics storage back to denied. A hit may already have been sent.

Email

If you email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com, we receive your address and your message so we can reply. We keep them only as long as needed for that reply and for a correction log.

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads once units are enabled. See how Google uses cookies in advertising. Cookies

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Google Analytics 4 may set a cookie only after you accept. The accept or reject choice is stored in local storage in your browser so the banner can stay dismissed. It stays on your device. Which Car Fit does not sell personal information.
